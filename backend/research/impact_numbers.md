# Impact & Scalability Metrics

---

1. **Indian Enterprise Compliance Footprint:** Over 2,500+ commercial banks, NBFCs, insurance firms, and SEBI-regulated market infrastructure entities operate under mandated RBI/SEBI Cybersecurity Framework guidelines requiring continuous cryptographic audit control.
2. **National Quantum Mission Alignment:** India's Cabinet approved the **National Quantum Mission (NQM)** with a total budget allocation of **₹6,003.65 Crore (2023–2031)** to build quantum hardware, communications, and cryptographic security standards. ECDAT directly supports NQM's quantum security pillar.
3. **CERT-In Advisory Mandate:** CERT-In advisories on PQC readiness mandate public sector entities and critical information infrastructure (CII) to perform baseline Cryptographic Bill of Materials (CBOM) audits.
4. **Enterprise Scalability Roadmap:** Expands from current 2-language core (Python + Java) to full CI/CD GitHub Actions / GitLab CI pipeline integration for continuous automated SDLC scanning across enterprise codebases.
